My personal research takes to create stories of people, using props, AI, and pictures I took.

"Found Archives" brings together works in which I construct archives that have never existed. I reconstruct photographs, diaries, Polaroids, drawings, rocks and other objects designed to look like authentic artefacts. Through alter egos and also using artificial intelligence, I explore universal themes such as our relationship with nature, loss and identity.

In addition to this, at the heart of the work there is always a question that has long interested me: our relationship with images and the assumption that they are true. And what happens when that relationship breaks down.
