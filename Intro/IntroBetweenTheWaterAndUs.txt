What's between the river and the city?

A project carried out as part of the Sense of Place course at Fondazione Studio Marangoni, presents the riverbank as a boundary situated between the river and the city.

I therefore explored the banks of the Arno in Florence in search of the traces left there by humanity, who often do so without even realising they are in such an important yet forgotten place of transition. The photographs, starting from the water and gradually moving towards the city, take the viewer on this journey.

This project has been selected as the cover of the Lungarno magazine for the month of November 2026, the 60th anniversary for the 1966 flood of the Arno River in Florence.
