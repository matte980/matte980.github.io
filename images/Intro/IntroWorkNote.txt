After my academic career, I have retained a passion for science and research, that I want to communicate through stories and images.

The works I love to follow are about the relationship between nature and us, could it be inside a laboratory, out in the field, or inside a large structure such as CERN.

Whether I'm in particle physics and accelerator laboratories or amongst the trees of a national park, looking for wolves, my aim is to show the work of researchers and the issues they study from a visual perspective.

I believe photography is an essential tool for science communication, reaching the audience where data alone cannot.
