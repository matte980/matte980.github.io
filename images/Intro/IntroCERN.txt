A photographic walk inside CERN, the European laboratory for particle physics in Geneva.

After having been selected among other 16 photographers from all over the world, in May 2025, I visited CERN, including the new tunnels for the next High-Luminosity Large Hadron Collider (HL-LHC), that will be operative in 2029.

I was supposed to go to CERN in 2020, during my Bachelor's thesis about CMS, but due to COVID I wasn't able to go there, so it has been amazing to finally go for the first time, not as a physicist but as a photographer.
