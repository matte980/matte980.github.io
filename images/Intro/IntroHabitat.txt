Notes on (co)habitation

Habitāre is the Latin word for "to live", "to reside", "to occupy a place". From it comes the Italian verb abitare, and also the word habitat, which we use for the specific environment in which a living being lives.

A landscape, after all, is never empty. It is inhabited by people, animals and plants, and by the culture and legends that have gathered there over time.

These projects explore the relationship between humans and nature as a form of coexistence, whether silent, conflictual or symbiotic, that runs through past, present and future.
