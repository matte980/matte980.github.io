Among the mountains to the East of Florence, young shepherd learn the job guided by experts.

In collaboration with the Casentinesi Forests National Park, this report documents ‘Pasturs’, a voluntary project in which young people spend at least two weeks living on a farm within the Park, assisting a shepherd with their daily work in exchange for board and lodging.

They learn sheep-herding techniques that are at risk of disappearing, and contribute to the project’s broader aim: to facilitate coexistence between livestock farmers and large predators, including wolves.
