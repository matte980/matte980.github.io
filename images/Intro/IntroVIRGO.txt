Inside Virgo, the three-kilometre-long gravitational-wave detector.

Virgo, the gravitational-wave detector at the European Gravitational Observatory in Cascina, near Pisa.

Together with its "collegues", LIGO in the United States and KAGRA in Japan, Virgo is part of the international network that, since 2015, has been observing the gravitational waves generated by events such as the fusion of black holes or neutron stars.
