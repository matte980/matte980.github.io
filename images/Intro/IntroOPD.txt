Have you ever wondered how experts restore an ancient masterpiece?

Inside the Opificio delle Pietre Dure in Florence, one of the world's oldest institutions dedicated to the restoration and conservation of art, researchers, restorers and conservationists work together to save some of the most ancient art masterpieces artists have ever created.

Founded in the 16th century by the Medici, the Opificio has evolved from a workshop for hardstone inlay (commesso fiorentino) into a leading center for the restoration of paintings, stone, mosaics and decorative arts, where craftsmanship and scientific research work side by side.
