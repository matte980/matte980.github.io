Have you ever met the "Uomo Selvatico"?

This project was created during a residential workshop in Lizzano in Belvedere, a village in the Tuscan-Emilian Apennines, in August this year. It is said that in the woods above the village lived a “wild man”, rough yet wise and learned, who is said to have passed on his knowledge of cheese-making and the art of chopping wood with metal axes to the people of Lizzano. It is said that the wild man is happy when it rains, because he knows the sun will be back, and sad when the sun is shining, because he knows the rain will return.
