The 1980s. A Chinese female astronaut travels to Mars alone on a space mission.

On the Red Planet, she sees something she shouldn’t have seen, so upon her return, the mission is covered up. She then falls into a personal crisis from which she will find it difficult to emerge.

This is a collection of the photographs she took, her diaries and her drawings.

The project takes this story as its starting point to examine our relationship with space and its exploration; our relationship with nature and the limits we face in that regard; and, finally, the trust we place in photographic images.
