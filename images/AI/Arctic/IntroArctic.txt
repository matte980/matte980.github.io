In the early years of the last century, a brave crew set sail for the Canadian Arctic in search of the famous Northwest Passage, with the aim of circumnavigating the Americas and reaching the East.

Unfortunately, the ship became trapped in the last of the summer ice and was subsequently destroyed when winter set in.
The survivors found themselves facing harsh weather conditions and a small Inuit community that seemed to harbour a huge and terrible secret.

These are some of the artefacts that have survived to the present day.
